Ministry of Road Transport (MoRTH)

Rule 50 of CMVR: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) has mandated High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) for all old and new vehicles registered in India.

Strict Compliance Penalty Notice:

Failure to fit certified HSRP license plates attracts heavy traffic violation fines ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 under recent RTO notifications.

Certified plates ensure safety, trace-ability against theft, and clean unified identification across country-wide toll points.

Frequently Asked Queries

1. Why is HSRP mandatory?

To curb vehicle duplication, theft and fake license plates. Each plate carries unique serial laser numbering mapped with central government vahan data.

2. What charges are involved in home delivery?

Currently, professional doorstep fitment is subsidized and set at ₹0 for home deliveries mapped during this window. Only nominal central plates pricing applies.

3. How long does the UTR check take?

Our system logs transaction details immediately. Physical cross-matching of accounts is fast and completes within 2-4 working hours.
